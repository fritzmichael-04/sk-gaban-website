About us

Youth leadership that serves the community.

The Sangguniang Kabataan is the governing body of the youth in the barangay. We plan and deliver programs for education, health, sports, culture, and civic participation, and we make sure young voices are heard in local governance.

Mission

To empower the youth of Gaban through programs that build skills, character, and a sense of civic responsibility.

Vision

A united, active, and responsible youth driving positive change in Barangay Gaban.

Values

Transparency, service, inclusivity, and accountability in everything we do.

What we do

Programs for every young Gabanian.

Our work is organized around the areas that matter most to the youth.

Education

Learning support, scholarships assistance, and skills workshops.

Sports and recreation

Leagues, tournaments, and fitness activities for all ages and abilities.

Health and environment

Wellness drives, clean-up activities, and tree planting.

Livelihood

Training and opportunities that prepare the youth for work and enterprise.

Culture and arts

Celebrating local talent, heritage, and creative expression.

Peace and governance

Youth participation in barangay decisions and community safety.
